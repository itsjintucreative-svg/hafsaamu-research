## 1. Choosing a Monthly Shopping Budget

The AIES Equal Profits system is designed to allow customers to select a participation amount based on their expected monthly shopping needs.

After registering on the Hafsaamu website and completing the required verification, eligible customers will be able to choose an available budget option, starting at ₹100 under the proposed initial structure.

The selected amount determines the customer's maximum eligible shopping value during the applicable 30-day period.

The intention is to allow customers to choose an option that reflects their normal monthly purchasing habits rather than selecting an amount unrelated to their actual needs.

## 2. How Equal Profits Eligibility Works

Under the proposed V2 structure, the monthly shopping eligibility limit is linked to the amount added to AIES.

For example:

- A customer who adds ₹100 can qualify for Equal Profits on up to ₹100 of eligible purchases during the 30-day period.
- A customer who adds ₹3,000 can qualify on up to ₹3,000 of eligible purchases.
- A customer who adds ₹10,000 can qualify on up to ₹10,000 of eligible purchases.

These amounts represent maximum eligible shopping values, not guaranteed profit amounts.

## 3. Profit Depends on Actual Eligible Shopping

The Equal Profits outcome depends on the value of qualifying purchases rather than simply the amount added to AIES.

Consider the illustrative example in which a ₹100 purchase produces a ₹20 profit outcome for the customer under the applicable model rules.

If the same rate applies proportionally to all eligible purchases, the examples would be:

| Actual eligible shopping | Illustrative customer profit |
|---|---:|
| ₹100 | ₹20 |
| ₹1,000 | ₹200 |
| ₹3,000 | ₹600 |
| ₹10,000 | ₹2,000 |

These figures illustrate a proportional 20% relationship based on the example provided. They are not a guarantee of returns, and the final calculation must follow the approved system rules.

## 4. What If a Customer Shops Below Their Selected Budget?

Customers will not need to spend their entire selected monthly budget to determine their actual eligible shopping value.

For example, a customer who selects the ₹10,000 option but makes only ₹3,000 in qualifying purchases during the applicable period would have their profit outcome calculated on the ₹3,000 of eligible purchases, subject to the final rules.

The selected amount establishes the eligibility ceiling. Actual qualifying purchases determine the value used to calculate the profit outcome.

This distinction is intended to help customers select an option aligned with their monthly shopping budget.

## 5. Why the Shopping Budget Matters

People have different purchasing needs and financial circumstances.

Some customers may spend only a few hundred rupees each month on qualifying purchases, while others may spend several thousand rupees.

AIES intends to offer different participation options so that customers can select an appropriate eligibility limit based on their expected shopping activity.

The objective is to make the system's participation structure understandable and aligned with actual purchasing needs.

Customers should not be encouraged to make unnecessary purchases or choose a higher budget than they can reasonably afford.

## 6. Customer Fund Withdrawals

Under the proposed customer policy, customers may request a withdrawal of their available funds, subject to the final terms and applicable requirements.

The intended processing time is approximately 12 hours, with a maximum stated processing window of 24 hours.

The final policy will need to clarify when this period begins, how requests are verified, what happens during technical or banking delays, and which funds are available for withdrawal.

The withdrawal policy must also clearly distinguish the customer's available balance from any amount already committed to transactions or otherwise subject to the system's rules.

## 7. Future Versions and Improvements

The 30-day eligibility period and the initial budget structure are proposed features of the current version.

As AIES develops, future versions—including V2 and V3 improvements—may introduce changes to eligibility periods, budget options, calculations, or other operating rules.

Any changes will depend on testing, economic feasibility, customer needs, and applicable legal requirements.

Customers should be informed clearly about the rules applicable to their version and any changes that affect their rights or participation.

## Conclusion

The proposed AIES customer system links a customer's selected monthly budget to their maximum eligible shopping value over a 30-day period.

The actual Equal Profits outcome is calculated according to qualifying purchases and the applicable model rules. Selecting a ₹10,000 option does not mean receiving ₹10,000 in profit.

Customers may also request withdrawal of available funds under the proposed withdrawal policy, with an intended processing window of 12 to 24 hours.

AIES will continue developing and evaluating this structure as the project progresses, with the objective of building a clear, sustainable, and responsibly operated Equal Profits system.
