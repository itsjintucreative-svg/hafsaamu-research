# AIES User Experience: From Discovering Local Businesses to Building a Digital Investment Portfolio

**A look inside the proposed customer-side experience of AIES — Hafsaamu's Economic Investment System.**

### Introduction

What if people could discover local businesses, understand their financial performance, explore their growth potential, and participate in business opportunities through a single digital platform?

This is one of the experiences AIES aims to develop.

AIES (AI Economic Investment System) is a research-stage project under Hafsaamu. Its long-term vision is to explore how technology, business financial data, machine learning, and intelligent economic allocation can work together to create a more connected business ecosystem.

To demonstrate this vision, we are developing a customer-side application prototype.

The proposed application is designed around a simple journey:

**Create an account → Discover businesses → Explore financial information → Understand business performance → Participate in investment opportunities → Monitor your portfolio.**

This article explains how the proposed user experience works, from the first application screen to portfolio management.

---

## 1. Getting Started: Creating an AIES Account

The user journey begins with a simple welcome screen.

Users can create an account or sign in to an existing account using their email address or phone number.

The onboarding experience is designed to be simple and accessible, without unnecessary complexity.

After registration, users proceed to profile setup.

## 2. Building a User Profile

Before accessing the complete platform, users will be able to configure their personal profiles.

The proposed profile setup includes three main sections.

**Personal Information**
- Full name
- Date of birth
- Residential address
- Preferred location

**Identity Verification**

A future version may support identity verification through appropriate documents and regulated verification processes.

**Banking and Payment Information**

Users may eventually be able to connect supported bank accounts or UPI details for eligible transactions and withdrawals, subject to applicable financial regulations and security requirements.

The objective is to create a structured account experience that can support future platform functionality.

*Note: These capabilities are planned features. The current demonstration does not collect or verify real identity or banking information.*

---

## 3. Home Dashboard: Discover Businesses Around You

After completing the onboarding experience, users arrive at the AIES home dashboard.

The dashboard is designed to make local business discovery easier.

Based on the user's selected location, AIES will display a list of businesses available in that area.

For example, a user selecting Silchar, Assam, could explore different business categories:

- Food and Beverage
- Retail Stores
- Grocery Shops
- Hospitality
- Local Services
- Other Businesses

Each business listing may display relevant information, such as:

- Business name and category
- Business location
- Historical financial performance
- Revenue and expense indicators
- Historical profitability
- Business activity status

The purpose is to help users understand businesses through structured information rather than relying entirely on advertisements or promotional descriptions.

Users can also explore businesses in other locations through the search feature.

---

## 4. Explore and Select Businesses

The Explore section provides a dedicated business discovery experience.

Users can search by:

- Business name
- Location
- Business category

They can browse different businesses, compare available information, and select businesses that interest them.

For example, a user may discover a local cafe, grocery store, or retail business and open its detailed profile.

The objective is to make business information accessible and easier to understand.

---

## 5. Business Profile: Understanding Financial Performance

Every participating business will have its own detailed profile.

This is one of the most important parts of the proposed AIES experience.

Instead of displaying only a business name and description, the platform aims to present structured business performance information.

A business profile may include:

**Business Overview**
- Business introduction
- Location and category
- Business activity
- Available financial indicators

**Financial History**
- Monthly revenue
- Operating expenses
- Net profit or loss
- Historical financial trends
- Business performance comparisons

This information will help users understand the historical performance of a business before considering any participation.

AIES aims to make business-related information more transparent and accessible.

---

## 6. Machine Learning Forecast: Understanding Future Possibilities

One of the planned premium capabilities of AIES is its Machine Learning Forecast module.

Traditional financial reports mainly explain what has already happened.

AIES aims to explore how machine learning can help analyze historical business data and estimate possible future business scenarios.

The proposed ML module may provide:

- Future revenue estimates
- Potential profitability trends
- Business performance forecasts
- Financial risk indicators
- Different prediction periods
- Model confidence and performance information

Users may be able to explore different forecast periods, such as:

- 1 Month
- 3 Months
- 6 Months
- 12 Months

For example, a user exploring a cafe could see its historical revenue trend alongside an illustrative machine learning forecast.

The goal is not to promise future profits.

The goal is to provide additional analytical information that can support better understanding and decision-making.

**Important:** ML forecasts are estimates, not guarantees. The actual predictive performance of AIES will need to be validated using real-world business data.

The Machine Learning Forecast is planned as a premium feature in the future commercial version.

---

## 7. Investment Interface: Participating in Business Opportunities

AIES is also exploring a proposed investment participation interface.

After reviewing a business profile and its available financial information, eligible users may eventually be able to explore participation opportunities.

The proposed interface includes:

- Selected business information
- Available participation amount
- Minimum participation amount
- Maximum permitted amount
- Investment summary
- Relevant terms and conditions

For demonstration purposes, the prototype uses ₹100 as an illustrative starting amount.

The actual minimum and maximum amounts would depend on the final business model, applicable legal framework, permissions, and the specific opportunity.

Before any real investment functionality is introduced, the platform would require appropriate legal and regulatory structuring, investor protections, payment infrastructure, and compliance processes.

No real investment is possible through the current prototype.

---

## 8. Investment Confirmation and Digital Records

In the proposed future application, a completed and legally supported transaction would generate a digital record.

The user experience is designed to include:

- Transaction reference
- Business details
- Transaction amount
- Transaction date
- Digital confirmation
- Relevant transaction documentation

Users may receive transaction-related documents through their registered email address.

A downloadable invoice or transaction statement would also be available within the application.

These records would help users maintain an organized history of their activities on the platform.

The current prototype only demonstrates this experience using fictional data.

---

## 9. My Assets: Managing Your Business Portfolio

After participating in eligible business opportunities, users would be able to monitor their activities through the My Assets section.

This section is designed as a personal portfolio dashboard.

It may display:

**Portfolio Overview**
- Total participation amount
- Number of active business positions
- Historical portfolio activity
- Business-wise allocation

**Individual Business Records**

Users can select any business in their portfolio to explore its available financial information and historical performance.

**Daily Monitoring**

Users may revisit the application to observe updated business information and relevant performance indicators as new verified data becomes available.

The objective is to create a centralized place where users can monitor their business-related participation.

---

## 10. Investment History and User Profile

The application will also include dedicated sections for personal account management.

**Investment History**

Users can review their previous transactions, digital records, and available business participation history.

**User Profile**

Users can manage their personal information, account settings, verification status, and supported payment details.

Together, these features aim to provide a complete and organized customer-side experience.

# How AIES Plans to Build This System

The customer application is only one part of the larger AIES architecture.

Behind the proposed experience, several development stages are required.

### Stage 1 — Research and Simulation

The initial development focuses on:

- Economic model research
- Synthetic business datasets
- Initial machine learning experiments
- Economic simulation
- Frontend application development

At this stage, the project is focused on demonstrating the concept and evaluating its technical feasibility.

### Stage 2 — Real Business Data Collection

The next major milestone is establishing relationships with local businesses.

AIES plans to approach selected shops and businesses to explore voluntary data-sharing partnerships.

Potential data categories include:

- Historical sales
- Monthly revenue
- Operating expenses
- Inventory information
- Business costs
- Profit and loss records
- Operational performance

Data collection would require appropriate consent, confidentiality arrangements, and privacy safeguards.

### Stage 3 — Machine Learning Development

Once sufficient real-world data becomes available, the existing research models can be evaluated and improved.

The development process will include:

1. Data collection and validation.
2. Data cleaning and preprocessing.
3. Feature engineering.
4. Model training and testing.
5. Prediction evaluation.
6. Comparison between synthetic-data and real-data performance.

The objective is to understand whether machine learning can produce useful and reliable business performance insights.

### Stage 4 — Pilot Development

After technical validation, AIES aims to explore a controlled pilot with selected businesses.

This stage will help evaluate:

- Actual business data integration
- Prediction performance
- User experience
- Operational feasibility
- Business adoption

### Stage 5 — Commercial Development

Following successful research, validation, and applicable regulatory approvals, AIES can explore its commercial product model.

Potential commercial areas include business analytics, premium ML forecasting, enterprise licensing, and other suitable services.

# Current Development Status

AIES is currently a research and simulation-stage project.

The customer-side application presented here is an interactive frontend demonstration of the proposed experience.

Current limitations:

- No real shops have been onboarded yet.
- No real business financial database is connected.
- Current business listings and financial figures are illustrative.
- Machine learning forecasts shown in the demonstration are simulated.
- No actual investments, withdrawals, or financial transactions are supported.
- Banking and identity verification are not operational.

We believe in presenting the development stage transparently rather than suggesting that planned capabilities are already operational.
