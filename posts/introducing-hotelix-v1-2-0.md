# Introducing Hotelix V1.2.0: Making Hotel Data More Useful

Running a hotel means making decisions every day — from staffing and budgeting to preparing for busy and quiet periods.

But many independent hotels already have something valuable that often goes underused: their own historical data.

That's where **Hotelix** comes in.

## Making Hotel Data More Useful

Hotelix is a local-first hotel forecasting and operational intelligence application developed by Hafsaamu.

It helps hotel owners work with their daily records, analyse historical performance, and explore future occupancy and revenue trends.

With Hotelix V1.2.0, users can:

- Enter hotel records manually or import data through CSV files.
- Analyse historical hotel performance.
- Explore occupancy and revenue forecasts.
- Understand potential high and low-demand periods.
- Support operational and budgeting decisions with data-driven insights.

## Built Around Your Own Data

Hotelix is designed as a Windows application, with hotel data processed locally rather than requiring it to be sent to an online platform.

The goal is simple: make hotel data easier to understand and more useful for everyday planning.

## What's Next?

Hotelix is still evolving. We're continuing to improve the application and explore how technology can make independent hotel operations more informed and efficient.

**Hotelix V1.2.0 is now available in Public Beta.**
