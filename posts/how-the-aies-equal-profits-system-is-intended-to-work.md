## 1. Introduction


AIES (Auto Income Eco System), operating through Hafsaamu, aims to develop an economic ecosystem based on its Equal Profits model.

![AIES Economic Model](/media/aies35.jpg)

Once the first group of participating shops has completed onboarding and the system is ready for its intended operations, eligible customers will be able to register on the Hafsaamu website and access the AIES Equal Profits system, subject to the applicable rules and conditions.

The system is designed around a different approach to economic participation: customers may become eligible for profit outcomes associated with their purchases without having to operate a shop or personally sell products.

## 2. Step One: Customer Registration

Customers will first need to register on the Hafsaamu website to access the AIES system.

The registration process may include:

- Creating a customer account.
- Providing the required personal information.
- Submitting the documents required for verification.
- Accepting the applicable terms and conditions.
- Completing any additional eligibility checks required by the system.

Access to Equal Profits will depend on successful registration and satisfaction of the relevant eligibility requirements.

## 3. Step Two: Adding Funds to AIES

After registration, eligible customers will be presented with available fund-contribution options.

Under the proposed initial structure, the minimum amount will be ₹100, with higher amounts available subject to the system's applicable limits and conditions.

Customers will select an amount and add the funds to their AIES account according to the approved process.

The intended role of these funds is to support the system's economic operations. AIES plans to coordinate the allocation, reinvestment, and movement of funds through participating businesses using its confidential internal model.

The exact allocation mechanism will remain confidential during development.

## 4. Step Three: Purchasing Products From Participating Shops

Once eligible, a customer may purchase products from participating shops in the AIES ecosystem, subject to the applicable purchase and eligibility rules.

For example, suppose a customer has added ₹100 to AIES and purchases a product worth ₹100 from an eligible shop.

Under the illustrative Equal Profits model, if the applicable terms specify a ₹20 profit outcome for that transaction, the customer may become eligible for that outcome alongside receiving the purchased product.

The actual eligibility, amount, timing, and settlement of any profit will depend on the final system rules. This example illustrates the intended concept and is not a guarantee of a return.

## 5. Understanding the Time-Based Eligibility Model

One of the proposed conditions of AIES is a time-based eligibility structure.

Consider a shopkeeper who starts a new shop with ₹100 worth of inventory. The time required to sell that inventory could be one day, two days, or as long as 30 days, depending on demand and other conditions.

AIES uses this type of question to frame its approach to eligibility periods: what time period should apply to a given level of economic participation?

For the proposed first version, V2, the initial period is planned to begin at 30 days.

Under this proposed structure, a customer who adds ₹100 may be eligible for up to ₹100 in qualifying purchases during the defined 30-day period, subject to the applicable terms.

Similarly, a customer who adds ₹10,000 may be eligible for up to ₹10,000 in qualifying purchases during that period.

These figures describe proposed eligibility limits, not the amount of profit guaranteed to a customer.

The exact calculation of profit, the conditions for receiving it, and the treatment of unused eligibility must be defined in the final rules.

## 6. Understanding the Customer's Economic Role

A central idea behind AIES is that customers may participate in the ecosystem's economic activity without personally operating a shop or selling products.

In a conventional business, a shopkeeper purchases inventory, sells products, manages expenses, and attempts to generate profit.

Under the proposed AIES model, a customer could participate through the system's defined fund and purchase eligibility arrangements rather than performing those business activities personally.

The AIES model would coordinate the relevant economic operations through participating businesses.

This does not mean that customers automatically become legal business owners or acquire ownership of participating shops. Their rights and responsibilities will depend on the final legal and operational structure.

## 7. A Version-Based Development Approach

The initial 30-day eligibility period is a proposed V2 rule, not necessarily a permanent feature.

As AIES develops, future versions may explore shorter eligibility periods or different arrangements, depending on testing, economic feasibility, operational performance, and applicable requirements.

Any change will need to be evaluated before implementation.

The objective is to develop a model that can operate consistently and sustainably rather than reduce eligibility periods simply for the sake of faster results.

## 8. Terms, Conditions, and Responsible Implementation

Before the system becomes operational, AIES will need to establish clear rules covering:

- Customer eligibility and document verification.
- The permitted use and allocation of customer funds.
- Purchase limits and applicable eligibility periods.
- The calculation and settlement of profit outcomes.
- Refunds, cancellations, losses, and disputed transactions.
- Withdrawal rights and the treatment of unused funds.
- Customer disclosures, privacy, and recordkeeping.
- The responsibilities of AIES and participating businesses.

The final terms must accurately describe how the system works and the financial risks involved.

Because the proposed model involves adding funds, allocating them through businesses, and providing profit outcomes, its legal classification and applicable Indian regulatory requirements must also be assessed before accepting funds or launching the system.

## Conclusion

The proposed AIES customer journey begins with registration on Hafsaamu, followed by verification, selection of a fund amount, and participation in qualifying purchases under the applicable terms.

The Equal Profits model is intended to allow eligible customers to participate in economic outcomes without personally operating a shop or selling products.

The proposed V2 structure introduces a 30-day eligibility period, with the eligible purchase amount linked to the amount added to AIES.

The confidential mechanism behind Equal Profits remains under development. Its financial sustainability, legal structure, and real-world performance must be established through appropriate testing and validation before the system can make reliable operational commitments.

**AIES aims to explore a different form of economic participation, built around the vision of Equal Profits.**
